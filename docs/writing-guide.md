# Writing guide

These are the style rules the skills follow. Change them to suit yourself; the skills read this file.

## Voice

- **Sound like you.** Your own phrasing beats polished phrasing. If a line sounds like a press release or a chatbot, rewrite it the way you'd say it to a colleague.
- **Implied first person.** Bullets start with a verb ("Led…", "Built…", "Drove…") and drop the "I". Don't write about yourself in the third person. The cover letter is the exception: it's a letter, so "I" is normal there.
- **Specific over impressive.** "Team of 12 across AWS and Azure, about $6M a year in spend" beats "large-scale multi-cloud leadership".
- **Past tense for past roles**, including a role you just left.

## Words to avoid

They make a resume read as generated or padded: *spearheaded, leveraged, utilized, synergy, robust, seamless, cutting-edge, best-in-class, dynamic, passionate about excellence, results-driven, proven track record, delve, tapestry, landscape, empower, unlock, elevate, harness, orchestrated, championed*.

## Punctuation

- **No em dashes** by default. Use a colon, a comma, parentheses or a new sentence. (Delete this rule if you like em dashes.)
- En dashes are fine in date ranges: `May 2022 – Oct 2026`.
- One space after a period. No trailing spaces.

## Honesty

- Every claim must be true and defensible in an interview.
- "Governed" a budget is not "owned" it. "Supported" is not "led". Use the verb that matches what you did.
- Mark light experience honestly ("Splunk (light use)").
- Numbers: use real ones, approximate with `~` or "about", and never round up. No numbers is better than invented numbers.

## Master resume versus tailored resume

| | Master (`master_resume.md`) | Tailored (`applications/.../resume.md`) |
|---|---|---|
| Purpose | Source of truth for your whole career | One job |
| Length | As long as it needs to be | Usually 2–3 pages |
| Detail | Everything true and useful | What matters for this job |
| Structure | Whatever reads well | Strict, applicant-tracking-friendly (see `ats-and-workday.md`) |
| Published? | Never, by default | Never |
